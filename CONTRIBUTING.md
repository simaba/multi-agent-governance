# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in improving this repository.

## Scope

This repository is a practical framework for governing multi-agent systems. Good contributions improve clarity, control, accountability, and operational usefulness.

## Good contributions

Examples of helpful contributions include:
- clarifying role definitions,
- improving escalation logic,
- adding realistic examples,
- strengthening governance diagrams,
- proposing additional design rules for control and debuggability.

## Contribution expectations

Please keep contributions:
- concise,
- implementation-aware,
- free of proprietary or confidential material,
- grounded in real system behavior rather than vague claims.

## Pull requests

When opening a pull request, explain:
- what changed,
- why it improves the framework,
- and whether it affects the conceptual model, examples, or templates.
